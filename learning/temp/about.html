ABOUT EDUKOM

Edukom is an online tutoring platform offering personalized and flexible education for students aged 5–18 years. Led by Ope Komolafe, the platform covers a range of subjects following different curricula. Edukom prepares students for exams, addressing logistical and security concerns through online classes with a team of experienced tutors fully dedicated to empowering students for global excellence and a lasting legacy.
