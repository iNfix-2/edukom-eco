Overview

General & Getting Started

How are tutors selected for the platform?

expand_more
Our tutors undergo a rigorous screening process, including evaluations, interviews, and competency-based recommendations to ensure they have the necessary qualifications, expertise, and experience in their respective fields.

What is the process for matching students with the right tutor?

expand_more
We carefully assess each student's learning requirements, academic goals, upcoming examinations, and preferred learning style to match them with a suitable tutor who possesses the relevant expertise and teaching approach to best meet their needs.

What subjects and grade levels do you offer tutoring for?

expand_more
We offer tutoring services for a wide range of subjects, including Mathematics, Add/Further Mathematics, English, Science, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Literature, Government, History, Geography, Information Communication Technology (ICT), Languages, and more, catering to students aged 5–18 years (from Reception to A-Levels/6th Form).

Do you offer exam preparatory classes?

expand_more
Yes, we do. We offer examination preparatory classes for IGCSE/GCSE, WASSCE, NECO, GCE, JAMB, SATs, IELTS, Junior WASSCE, Cambridge Checkpoint, Common Entrance, and more.

Do you provide home tutoring services?

expand_more
If there are extenuating circumstances that make it impossible or unwise to conduct online classes productively, special arrangements can be made for face-to-face tutoring at a location provided by the parent or guardian.

Parents & Guardians

For Parents & Students

How do you track and communicate student progress to parents or guardians?

expand_more
We have an ongoing feedback relationship with the parents/guardians of the learners. This helps us to track improvements, challenges, upcoming assessments, and need for redesign of teaching methods. The openness of our communication makes it easy to stay on track and consolidate academic gains.

Is there a restriction to scheduling of lessons due to different time zones?

expand_more
No, there are no restrictions by time zone. Aside from having global tutors available across multiple continents, we have tutors expressly available to teach at any allocated time within a 24-hour cycle.

What is the procedure for scheduling and rescheduling sessions?

expand_more
Students and parents can easily schedule or reschedule tutoring sessions through our platform or by contacting our support team, ensuring convenience and flexibility for any schedule updates.

How are classes conducted?

expand_more
Currently, at Edukom we use Zoom, Google Meet, Google Classroom etc. All classes are highly interactive, engaging, and structured to maximize learner participation.

How many students do you have in a session?

expand_more
One tutor, one student per class (1-on-1). One of our core commitments is attending to the unique learning needs of each child without divided tutor attention.

What happens if our family relocates?

expand_more
Nothing! Our classes are conducted online, allowing your child to seamlessly continue learning from wherever you relocate in the world.

Tutors & Educators

For Tutors

How do I apply as a tutor with Edukom?

expand_more
Visit our website and fill out the tutor application form with your current CV. Qualified candidates will be scheduled for a virtual assessment and interview, followed by an onboarding orientation.

Who pays for the internet data used for teaching?

expand_more
Both sides cover their respective data costs. Class-related expenses are factored into tutor compensation paid at the conclusion of each monthly cycle.

If a tutor cannot be available for a class, what should they do?

expand_more
Notify Edukom Admin immediately. The tutor will coordinate with the parent to arrange an alternate time to make up the session within the same payment cycle.

How do Tutors get paid?

expand_more
Tutors are compensated per hour of tutoring. At the end of each month, verified hours are calculated and paid via direct bank transfer.

Is there a maximum number of hours a Tutor can take per month?

expand_more
There is no maximum hour cap, provided teaching quality remains high and scheduled sessions do not clash.

Pricing

Billing & Class Costs

How much do the classes cost?

expand_more

Tutoring costs are tailored specifically to each learner based on several key factors:

    1. The academic level/class of the child 2. The curriculum being run (British, Nigerian, American, IPC) 3. The age and specific educational needs 4. Specialized exam preparation requirements (IGCSE, WASSCE, SAT, etc.) 5. Time zone differences and scheduling commitments

During the tutor booking process, your customized fee estimate will be communicated clearly before final confirmation.
