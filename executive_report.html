2. Key Upgrades Delivered by Operational Area

A. Virtual Classrooms & Scheduling Completed
  • Embedded Video Rooms: High-definition video with in-room chat, screen sharing, hand-raising, and live digital whiteboard.
  • No Link Sharing: Rooms are private and token-guarded; students enter directly from their dashboard.
  • Double-Booking Guard: Prevents tutor and student timetable clashes automatically.
  • Dual-Timezone Clock: Displays both local time and West Africa Time (WAT) for diaspora students in the UK, US, and UAE.
  • Calendar Sync: 1-click export to Google, Apple, and Outlook calendars via .ics feed.
B. Automated Payroll & Bank KYC Completed
  • Zero-CSV Direct Bridge: Operations can generate an entire monthly payroll run directly from completed lessons with 1 click.
  • 140 Compensation Tiers: Automatically calculates exact earnings across subjects, curricula, and experience levels.
  • Instant NUBAN Bank Resolution: Validates Nigerian bank accounts in real time against official banking databases.
  • Self-Service Payslips: Tutors can view, verify, and download itemized compensation statements on their dashboard.
  • Cybersecurity Shield: Formulas in spreadsheets are automatically neutralized against security vulnerabilities (CWE-1236).
C. Anti-Piracy Video Library & Studio Completed
  • Encrypted Video Player (DRM): High-grade AES-128 encryption prevents unauthorized ripping and screen downloading.
  • Concurrent Stream Guard: Restricts accounts to 1 device at a time, eliminating illegal credential sharing.
  • Dynamic Watermark: Displays the viewer's registered name and IP address on screen during playback to deter leaks.
  • Adaptive Data Saver Mode: Automatically adjusts video quality (down to 360p) for smooth playback on mobile networks.
  • Creator Studio: Instructors can create video courses, structure modules, and upload worksheets easily.
D. WhatsApp & SMS Automations Completed
  • 3-Tier Multi-Stakeholder Reminders: Automated reminders sent at 24 hours, 1 hour, and 10 minutes before class to students, tutors, and parents.
  • Subscription Expiry Alerts: Proactive renewal reminders sent 3 days and 1 day prior to package expiration.
  • Two-Way Parent Triage: Parents can reply to WhatsApp reminders; replies are logged and urgent keywords (e.g. cancel, emergency) trigger immediate alerts to staff.
  • Admin Operations Hub: Real-time monitoring dashboard with queue inspection and test simulation modes.
  • Parent Quiet Hours: Respects family privacy by suppressing non-urgent notifications during night hours.
E. Dedicated Portals & Academic Tools Completed
  • Guardian Portal: Multi-child switcher allowing parents to track attendance, grades, and tutor notes for each child.
  • Student Homework Drawer: Students submit homework assignments (essays & files); tutors grade directly in their portal.
  • Interactive Quizzes: Instant self-grading multiple choice quizzes with detailed explanations.
  • Verifiable Digital Certificates: Branded graduation certificates with unique QR codes for public verification.
  • Tutor Hub: Daily teaching schedule, pending grading alerts, and accrued earnings metrics.
F. Mobile-First UX & Guided Onboarding Completed
  • Mobile Bottom Navigation Dock: Native app-like quick access to Home, Courses, Get Tutor, and Videos.
  • Guided 4-Step Onboarding: Interactive progress bar guiding new students and tutors through account setup.
  • Classroom Hardware Diagnostic: Built-in microphone and camera tester ensuring zero technical hitches during class.
  • Returning User Hub: Welcome-back streak tracking and a 1-click countdown button directly into today's next lesson.
  • Branded Sign-In Redesign: Clean, responsive sign-in and sign-up interfaces across LMS and HR portals.

3. System Health & Production Readiness
